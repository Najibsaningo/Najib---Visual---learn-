🔎 Research & Online Services — 20 Lessons

Tap each lesson.

Lesson 1 — What Is Online Research?

Definition: Finding, checking and organising accurate information for someone who needs it.
Simple: You become the person who finds truth fast.
Practice: Find answer to 1 question in 10 mins, show sources.

Lesson 2 — Why People Pay for Research

Reason: Business owners, students, writers have no time. They pay for clear, correct research.
Practice: List 5 people who need research help.

Lesson 3 — What Is Virtual Assistance?

Definition: Helping businesses remotely: email, scheduling, data, social media, research.
Practice: List 10 VA tasks you can already do.

Lesson 4 — Asking the Right Question

Rule: Vague question = vague answer. Ask: What exactly? For who? By when? Format?
Practice: Turn 3 vague requests into clear questions.

Lesson 5 — Searching Like a Pro

Tricks: Use quotes "exact phrase", site:.go.ke, filetype:pdf, minus -word to exclude.
Practice: Try 5 advanced searches.

Lesson 6 — Checking If Source Is True

Check: Who wrote? When? Evidence? Other sources agree? Bias?
Practice: Check 3 news stories with this list.

Lesson 7 — Primary vs Secondary Sources

Primary: Original: interview, survey, official data. Secondary: Someone explaining primary.
Practice: Find 2 of each on 1 topic.

Lesson 8 — Organising Research

System: Folder per project, doc with Question / Findings / Sources / Summary.
Practice: Create template and use it.

Lesson 9 — Summarising: Say It Short

Rule: 1 page of research → 5 bullet points + 1 paragraph summary. Client wants answer, not 20 pages.
Practice: Summarise 1 article in 5 bullets.

Lesson 10 — Data: Finding Numbers

Sources: KNBS, World Bank, government sites, company reports. Always note date + source.
Practice: Find 5 statistics for 1 topic.

Lesson 11 — Making Simple Surveys

Steps: Goal → 5-10 clear questions → Share → Collect → Summarise.
Practice: Make 1 Google Form survey with 5 questions.

Lesson 12 — Interviews: Asking Well

Tips: Open questions, listen, don't lead, record with permission, thank them.
Practice: Interview 1 person, write summary.

Lesson 13 — Competitor Research

Ask: Who are competitors? Prices? Strengths? Weaknesses? Gaps?
Practice: Research 3 competitors for 1 business.

Lesson 14 — Market Research for Small Business

Check: Is there demand? Who buys? What price? Where?
Practice: Research 1 business idea fully.

Lesson 15 — Presenting Research Clearly

Format: Title → Question → Key Findings (bullets) → Details → Sources → Recommendation.
Practice: Present 1 research in this format.

Lesson 16 — Email & Communication as VA

Rules: Reply fast, be polite, confirm tasks, update daily, never disappear.
Practice: Write 3 professional email templates.

Lesson 17 — Time Management & Tools

Tools: Google Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Trello, Canva. Learn them well.
Practice: Organise 1 week with Calendar + Trello.

Lesson 18 — Pricing Your Services

Start: Research report KSh 500-1500, VA KSh 200-500/hour. Grow as you get reviews.
Practice: Make your price list.

Lesson 19 — Avoiding Scams in Online Work

Red flags: Pay to get job, free work forever, no contract, stolen data, fake checks.
Practice: Check 3 job posts against list.

Lesson 20 — Final Project: Deliver 1 Real Research Report

Task: Pick 1 business, do competitor + market research, deliver 2-page report with sources + recommendation.
Done? You can now offer research + VA services.
