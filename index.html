📚 Academic Writing - FREE COURSE

100% FREE lessons. You only pay KSh 50 if you want the Assessment / Certificate.

Lesson 1 - What is Academic Writing?

Structured writing used to communicate ideas, evidence and research clearly. It is formal, clear, and based on evidence, not just opinions.

Lesson 2 - Research Basics

1. Find a clear question
2. Find reliable information
3. Take notes in your own words
4. Organize notes before you write

Lesson 3 - Structure

Introduction: what you will discuss
Main sections: your points + evidence
Conclusion: what you learned
One main idea per paragraph

Lesson 4 - Referencing

When you use someone else's idea, say where it came from. This builds trust and avoids plagiarism.

Lesson 5 - Proofreading

Check clarity, grammar, spelling and structure. Read out loud - if it sounds confusing, rewrite it.
