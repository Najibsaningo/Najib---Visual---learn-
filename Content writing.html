✍️ Content Writing — 20 Lessons

Tap each lesson.

Lesson 1 — What Is Content Writing?

Definition: Writing useful, clear content for websites, social media and businesses.
Simple: You help businesses explain + sell with words.
Practice: List 5 businesses that need writing.

Lesson 2 — Why Businesses Pay Writers

Reason: Good writing brings customers. Bad writing loses them.
Practice: Find 1 bad business post, rewrite it better.

Lesson 3 — Knowing Reader Before Writing

Ask: Who is reading? What do they want? What do they fear?
Practice: Write reader profile for 1 business.

Lesson 4 — Finding Ideas

Sources: Customer questions, comments, Google, competitors, your life.
Practice: Write 20 ideas for 1 niche.

Lesson 5 — Headlines That Get Clicks

Formula: Number + Benefit + Curiosity. "7 Ways to..."
Practice: Write 10 headlines for 1 topic.

Lesson 6 — Introductions That Hook

Formula: Problem + Promise + Preview. 3 sentences max.
Practice: Write 3 intros for same topic.

Lesson 7 — Structure: Easy to Read

Rule: Short paragraphs, headings, lists, bold key points. People scan, not read.
Practice: Rewrite long paragraph into scan-friendly format.

Lesson 8 — Writing in Simple English

Rule: Short sentences. Simple words. One idea per sentence.
Practice: Rewrite 10 difficult sentences simply.

Lesson 9 — Storytelling in Writing

Formula: Person → Problem → Struggle → Solution → Lesson.
Practice: Tell your story in this format.

Lesson 10 — Blog Articles

Structure: Title → Intro → 3-5 points with examples → Conclusion → Call to action.
Practice: Write 1 blog of 500 words.

Lesson 11 — Social Media Captions

Formula: Hook (line 1) → Story/Value → Lesson → Question / CTA.
Practice: Write 5 captions.

Lesson 12 — Product Descriptions

Rule: Don't list features, show benefits. "Long battery" → "Use all day without charging".
Practice: Write descriptions for 3 products.

Lesson 13 — Website Pages: Home & About

Home: Who you help + How + Proof + CTA. About: Story + Values + Proof.
Practice: Write Home page for 1 business.

Lesson 14 — SEO Writing Basics

Rule: Use keyword in title, first paragraph, headings — naturally, not stuffed.
Practice: Write 1 SEO article with 1 keyword.

Lesson 15 — Research for Writers

Steps: Search → Read 3 sources → Note facts → Write in own words → Reference.
Practice: Research 1 topic, write 300 words.

Lesson 16 — Editing Your Own Work

Checklist: Cut 20% words, fix grammar, check clarity, read out loud.
Practice: Edit yesterday's work, cut 20%.

Lesson 17 — Writing in Different Voices

Voice = Formal vs friendly vs funny. Match business voice.
Practice: Write same post in 3 voices.

Lesson 18 — Avoiding Plagiarism & AI Copying

Rule: Never copy. Use AI for ideas, not final work. Your voice matters.
Practice: Rewrite 1 AI paragraph in your voice.

Lesson 19 — Finding Clients & Pricing

Start: Write 5 free samples → Show portfolio → Charge per article: KSh 500-2000 start, grow with skill.
Practice: Make portfolio + price list.

Lesson 20 — Final Project: 5-Piece Portfolio

Task: 1 Blog, 1 Caption set, 1 Product description, 1 Home page, 1 SEO article. All edited, all yours.
Done? You can now apply for writing work.
