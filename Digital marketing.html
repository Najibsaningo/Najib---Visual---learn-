📢 Digital Marketing — 20 Lessons

Tap each lesson to open it.

Lesson 1 — What Is Digital Marketing?

Definition: Promoting products/services using internet channels.
Simple: Helping right people find right product online.
Practice: List 5 ads you saw today — where did you see them?

Lesson 2 — Why Businesses Need It

Reason: Customers are online. If business is not visible, it loses sales.
Practice: Pick 1 local shop, explain how digital can help it.

Lesson 3 — The 4 Pillars

Pillars: Audience, Content, Channel, Measurement. If one fails, marketing fails.
Practice: Write these 4 for a small shop.

Lesson 4 — Knowing Your Audience Deeply

Ask: Age? Problem? Where do they spend time? What do they want?
Practice: Create 1 audience profile.

Lesson 5 — Customer Journey

Journey: See → Like → Trust → Buy → Return. Marketing guides each step.
Practice: Map journey for 1 product.

Lesson 6 — Content Marketing

Definition: Helpful content that attracts and builds trust.
Practice: Write 10 helpful content ideas for your niche.

Lesson 7 — Social Media Marketing

Rule: Each platform is different. TikTok = short video, Instagram = visual, WhatsApp = trust.
Practice: Choose 1 main platform + why.

Lesson 8 — How Algorithms Work (Simple)

Truth: Platforms show what people watch, like, share, save. Make content people finish.
Practice: Analyse 3 viral posts — why did people finish them?

Lesson 9 — Hooks: First 3 Seconds

Formula: Question / Shocking fact / Problem / Promise.
Example: "Stop wasting money on..."
Practice: Write 10 hooks.

Lesson 10 — Copywriting: Words That Sell

Formula: Problem → Agitate → Solution → Call to action.
Practice: Write 1 caption using this formula.

Lesson 11 — Visuals: Images & Video

Rule: Clear, bright, one message per image. Text must be readable on phone.
Practice: Design 1 poster for a product.

Lesson 12 — SEO Basics

Definition: SEO = making Google show your content when people search.
Practice: Find 10 keywords people search in your niche.

Lesson 13 — Email & WhatsApp Marketing

Rule: Permission first, value always, spam never.
Practice: Write 3 helpful broadcast messages.

Lesson 14 — Paid Ads: Basics

Definition: You pay platform to show your post to more people. Start small: KSh 200-500 test.
Practice: Plan 1 ad: Audience, budget, goal.

Lesson 15 — Landing Pages

Definition: One page, one goal. No distractions.
Practice: Sketch 1 landing page on paper.

Lesson 16 — Measuring Results

Track: Reach, clicks, messages, sales, cost. If you don't measure, you guess.
Practice: Make a tracking sheet.

Lesson 17 — Analytics: What Numbers Mean

Example: High views + low sales = wrong audience or weak offer. Low views = weak hook.
Practice: Analyse 1 post with numbers.

Lesson 18 — Branding: Why People Remember You

Brand = Colours + Voice + Promise + Consistency. Be same everywhere.
Practice: Choose your 2 colours, 1 voice, 1 promise.

Lesson 19 — Marketing Ethics & Scams

Never: Fake results, fake reviews, pressure, lying. Trust is your business.
Practice: Rewrite 1 hype ad into honest ad.

Lesson 20 — Final Project: Run a Real Campaign

Task: Pick 1 business, 1 audience, 1 platform. Create 7 posts + 1 ad plan + tracking sheet. Run for 14 days, report results.
Done? You understand Digital Marketing deeply.
