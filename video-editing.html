🎬 Video Editing — 20 Lessons

Tap each lesson.

Lesson 1 — What Is Video Editing?

Definition: Choosing, cutting and arranging clips, sound and text to tell a clear story.
Simple: Editing is storytelling, not just cutting.
Practice: Watch 1 video, write its story in 3 sentences.

Lesson 2 — Why Editing Matters

Truth: Good editing keeps people watching. Bad editing makes them swipe.
Practice: Compare 1 boring vs 1 engaging video — what was different?

Lesson 3 — Tools: Free to Start

Start with: CapCut, VN, InShot (phone). Later: Premiere, DaVinci.
Practice: Install 1 app, explore all buttons.

Lesson 4 — Planning Before Editing

Steps: Idea → Script → Shots → Edit. Never edit without plan.
Practice: Write script for 30-sec video.

Lesson 5 — Types of Shots

Shots: Wide, Medium, Close-up, B-roll. Use mix to keep interesting.
Practice: Film same thing in 3 shots.

Lesson 6 — Importing & Organising

Rule: Name folders: Project / Raw / Music / Export. Saves hours.
Practice: Organise your next project this way.

Lesson 7 — Cutting: The Core Skill

Rule: Cut silence, mistakes, boring parts. Keep only what moves story.
Practice: Cut a 2-min clip to 30 sec, still clear.

Lesson 8 — Pacing & Rhythm

Rule: Fast cuts = energy. Slow cuts = calm. Match pace to mood.
Practice: Edit same clip fast + slow, compare feeling.

Lesson 9 — Transitions: Less Is More

Rule: Use simple cuts 90% of time. Fancy transitions distract.
Practice: Edit 1 video using only cuts.

Lesson 10 — Text & Captions

Rule: Big, clear, 3-5 words per line, on screen 2-3 sec. Most watch on mute.
Practice: Add captions to 1 video.

Lesson 11 — Hooks: First 3 Seconds

Formula: Show result first, then how. "Watch how I..."
Practice: Create 5 hooks for 1 video.

Lesson 12 — B-Roll & Covering Mistakes

Definition: B-roll = extra clips over main talking. Hides cuts, adds beauty.
Practice: Add 5 B-roll clips to 1 talking video.

Lesson 13 — Music & Sound

Rules: Music low under voice, no copyrighted music for clients. Use free libraries.
Practice: Find 5 free songs for different moods.

Lesson 14 — Voice & Audio Cleaning

Tips: Record quiet room, close windows, use phone mic close. Remove noise in app.
Practice: Record same line noisy vs quiet.

Lesson 15 — Colour: Simple Correction

Steps: Brightness → Contrast → Warmth. Don't overdo. Natural looks professional.
Practice: Fix 1 dark video.

Lesson 16 — Thumbnails & Titles

Rule: 1 face + 3 words + bright colour = clicks. Must be true to video.
Practice: Design 3 thumbnails for 1 video.

Lesson 17 — Export Settings

Rule: TikTok/Reels: 1080x1920, 30fps. YouTube: 1920x1080. Keep quality high.
Practice: Export same video for TikTok + YouTube.

Lesson 18 — Editing for Clients

Rules: Ask: Goal? Audience? Deadline? Style? Always save original + get feedback.
Practice: Write client questions list.

Lesson 19 — Building Portfolio

Rule: No clients? Edit for free for 3 people, show before/after. Portfolio = proof.
Practice: Make 5 portfolio videos.

Lesson 20 — Final Project: Edit 3 Real Videos

Task: 1 TikTok (30s), 1 YouTube (3 min), 1 Client ad (60s). All with hook, captions, music, thumbnail.
Done? You can now charge for editing.
