🔗 Affiliate Marketing — 20 Lessons

Tap each lesson. Learn Definition → Simple Meaning → Example → Practice.

Lesson 1 — What Is Affiliate Marketing?

Definition: You recommend a product, someone buys through your link, you earn commission.
Simple: You are a trusted recommender, not the seller.
Example: You recommend a book you love, 10 people buy via your link, you get KSh 100 each.
Practice: Write 3 products you truly use and would recommend.

Lesson 2 — How Does It Actually Work?

Steps: 1. Join program 2. Get unique link 3. Share honestly 4. Person buys 5. You get paid.
Practice: Draw this 5-step flow on paper.

Lesson 3 — Commission, Cookie & Tracking

Definition: Commission = your pay. Cookie = small file that tracks who sent buyer. Usually lasts 7-30 days.
Practice: Compare 3 programs: commission %, cookie days.

Lesson 4 — Choosing a Niche

Definition: Niche = one topic you focus on.
Rule: Don't sell everything. Pick one: e.g. study tools, phones, skincare, football.
Practice: Pick 1 niche you know + love.

Lesson 5 — Choosing Honest Products

Rule: Only promote what you have tried or researched deeply. Lying kills trust.
Practice: List 5 products in your niche, rate them 1-5 honestly.

Lesson 6 — Finding Affiliate Programs

Where: Jumia, Amazon Associates, Impact, ShareASale, local shops with referral programs.
Practice: Find 3 real programs for your niche.

Lesson 7 — Your Audience: Who Are You Helping?

Definition: Audience = people with a problem you can solve.
Example: Students who need cheap study tools.
Practice: Write: Age, problem, what they need.

Lesson 8 — Trust: Why People Buy From You

Rule: People buy from people they trust. Show your face, show real results, admit weaknesses.
Practice: Write 1 honest review: 3 pros + 2 cons.

Lesson 9 — Content That Sells Without Lying

Types: How-to, comparison, review, tutorial, "best for..."
Practice: Write 5 content ideas for your niche.

Lesson 10 — Writing a Real Review

Formula: Who is it for? What I liked? What I didn't? Who should NOT buy? Final verdict.
Practice: Write 1 full review in this format.

Lesson 11 — Comparison Posts

Formula: Product A vs B: Price, Features, Best for, Verdict.
Practice: Compare 2 products in your niche.

Lesson 12 — Using WhatsApp, TikTok & Instagram

Rule: Give value first, link second. 80% help, 20% promote.
Practice: Plan 7 posts: 5 helpful, 2 with link.
